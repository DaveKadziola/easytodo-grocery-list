{# The dialog that moves a category, with its items, to another board: Alpine component "moveCategory" (static/js/board.js), outside the board content. The category menu's "Move to board…" opens it with the category's id and name (the "move-category" event on the window); the name is as text (x-text, .value), never written into a script. It is shown and hidden with its hidden attribute ("closed"), as the item modal is (see _item_modal.html), and while it is open the page behind is inert. A name that is taken on the target board stays in the dialog: the field gets the free name the server suggests, and the alert says why, until another board is picked. Esc, or a tap on the dimmed page, closes it; Tab keeps to its controls. Context: move_targets (the BoardChoice list of the boards to move to, not empty). -#}
