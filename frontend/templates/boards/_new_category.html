{# The dialog that adds a category: Alpine component "newCategory" (static/js/board.js), outside the board content, for a member who may edit. The "New category" buttons (the board header's, and the phone's bottom bar's) open it with an "open-new-category" event on the window; it asks for the name and whether the category goes on top or at the bottom (radio buttons: Tab enters the group once, the arrow keys move within it). What was typed stays through Cancel, Esc and a tap beside the dialog (a stray tap on a phone must not cost the name), and goes only once the category is added. The request swaps the new section into #categories, which the answer's HX-Reswap header puts first for "On top"; a name that is taken (409) or refused (422) leaves the dialog open with what was typed, and one the board refuses (403, 404) closes it, so that the reload that follows can run. Only the answer to the request of the dialog's current opening counts (an older one lands in a page that is no longer waiting for it). It is shown and hidden with its hidden attribute ("closed"), as the item modal is (see _item_modal.html), and while it is open the page behind is inert and live updates wait. Esc, or a tap on the dimmed page, closes it; Tab keeps to its controls. The panel has tabindex -1 so that Esc is still heard after a click on its text. Context: view (BoardView). -#}
