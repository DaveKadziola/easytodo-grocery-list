{# The item dialog of a member who may not edit: Alpine component "itemViewer" (static/js/board.js), on an element around the board content, like "itemModal" for an editor, and shown, closed, held in place and focused the same way (see _item_modal.html). It has no form and no field: board.js writes the name and the description (as text) from the data-* attributes of the item name that was clicked, and fetches the details into the slot. Esc or a tap on the dimmed page closes it, and Tab keeps to its controls. -#}
